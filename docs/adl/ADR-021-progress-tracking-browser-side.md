# ADR-021: Long-term progress tracking — browser-side, lost on cache clear

**Status:** Shell
**Date:** 2026-08-12

## Problem

Students may want long-term progress: which questions they answered correctly, attempt counts, score history over time. Without accounts (ADR-009) that data can only live in the browser, where it is lost on cache clear, in private browsing, and on a device change.

No decision has been made on whether to build it, what the UX is, or how the data-loss risk is communicated to the user.
